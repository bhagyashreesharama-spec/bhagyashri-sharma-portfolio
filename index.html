How I approach ideas

From a spark to something real.

A simple creative process that helps me turn a starting idea into a polished result.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand

Clarify the idea, audience, goal and feeling the final result should communicate.

02 / IMAGINE

Explore

Research inspiration, experiment with concepts and choose a visual direction.

03 / CREATE

Build

Bring the concept to life through design, AI tools, video or website code.

04 / REFINE

Improve

Review details, check usability and refine the work for a stronger result.
